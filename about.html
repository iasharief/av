About Isaac

A campaign built around long-term American strength.

Isaac Sharief is running with American Vanguard because the country deserves politics that can think past the next four years.

Why Isaac is running.

American Vanguard was built around the idea that America should lead the world again while solving the problems that make us weaker at home: debt, slow permitting, fragile supply chains, outdated defense planning, and an immigration system that punishes people for following the rules.

Isaac's case is not that government should do everything. It is that government should stop slowing down the work America needs, then get serious where strength actually depends on public action: defense, infrastructure, nuclear energy, AI, manufacturing, and secure borders.

The campaign's plain rules

Lead with purpose

Force needs a transparent goal, a real reason, and a clear exit.

Build what we need

Tax relief should reward companies investing and hiring inside the United States.

Respect the budget

Debt and interest payments are not boring math. They are a national security problem.

Help make the serious case.

The campaign needs supporters who can make the case clearly, directly, and with confidence.
