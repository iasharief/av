The Platform

Power used with purpose.

American Vanguard is running on long-term strength: a serious economy, a modern military, secure borders, faster legal immigration, and leadership that keeps America in control of its future.

Economic Vision

Reward businesses that manufacture, hire, invest, and innovate in the United States. Cut waste and focus spending on strength at home.

Bullet points
  • Targeted tax cuts for small businesses, growing companies, and firms building inside the US.
  • Reduce regulations slowing construction, energy production, manufacturing, and technology work.
  • Support low general tariffs with allies and trading partners to avoid raising family costs.
  • Use tariffs and tax incentives to protect semiconductors, medicine, defense materials, nuclear energy, aerospace, and AI.
  • Expand grants, loans, paid internships, and training in AI, cybersecurity, and defense technology.
  • Tax companies that use agentic AI to replace large numbers of workers without retraining, transition, or reinvestment.
  • Address the debt by cutting wasteful programs, auditing defense contracts, and focusing spending on long-term strength.

Domestic Policy

Build a country strong enough to lead: energy, manufacturing, exports, secure borders, and a legal immigration system that works.

Bullet points
  • Support small businesses through tax relief and easier access to loans.
  • Expand domestic manufacturing in technology, energy, defense, and medicine.
  • Invest in nuclear energy, natural gas, and clean technology to lower reliance on external countries.
  • Support EVs and cleaner transportation.
  • Support AI when it helps American workers instead of replacing them.
  • Secure the border with smart technology and higher presence to detect illegal crossings and cartel activity.
  • Speed up legal immigration, prioritize skilled workers and students, and create a path to legal status for undocumented immigrants residing in the United States.

Foreign Policy

Lead with allies, compete with hostile powers, protect the dollar, and avoid reckless nation-building.

Bullet points
  • Assertive foreign policy that spreads democratic ideals.
  • Lower reliance on foreign imports and keep America the leading economy.
  • Focus on drone warfare, targeted strikes, cyber attacks, and indirect influence rather than nation-building.
  • Ensure the US dollar remains the currency of the world.
  • End the proliferation of WMDs.
  • Win over nations in Africa, the Middle East, and Asia while curbing Chinese influence, especially in Africa and Latin America.
  • Reinforce US allies, expand NATO, and encourage allies to increase defense spending.
  • Combat BRI, avoid admitting BRI members to NATO, and encourage current NATO countries to leave the initiative.
  • Lower tariffs on countries that leave BRI and support infrastructure in such countries.
  • Reaffirm sanctions on anti-democratic states and SSOTs while protecting sovereignty.

Military

A strong military should deter hostile powers and be ready for future threats, not stuck preparing for the last war.

Bullet points
  • Increase investment in drones, cyber defense, missile defense, AI, naval power, and space security.
  • Modernize around future threats instead of large ground wars.
  • Use drones, cyber operations, and targeted strikes to reduce risk to American troops.
  • Require uses of force to have a clear mission and a direct reason.
  • Remove inefficient or cost-ineffective programs.
  • Strengthen recruitment with more incentives and support for families.
  • Audit the Department of Defense so every dollar spent is useful.

Technology and Innovation

Make America harder to depend on foreign competitors and easier to build in.

Bullet points
  • Invest in AI, cybersecurity, semiconductors, and other modern fields.
  • Open paid internships in AI and other fields that need a larger workforce.
  • Increase resources to develop more electric car batteries.
  • Create paid pathways for high school graduates to earn loan relief, down payment credit, and job experience in critical industries.
  • Support AI when it strengthens workers, improves national defense, or helps America advance.
  • Expand domestic semiconductor and battery production.

Immigration

Secure borders and a faster legal system belong together. The country needs enforcement and a lawful process that can actually function.

Bullet points
  • Secure the border with stronger surveillance and smart technology.
  • Crack down on cartel activity, human trafficking, and opioid smuggling.
  • Increase immigration judges and officers to speed up lines.
  • Speed up green card, asylum, and citizenship processing for those who follow our laws.
  • Reform country caps that create extreme wait times.
  • Prioritize skilled workers and students who can strengthen the US economy.
  • Protect asylum claims and process them quickly.
  • Create a path for undocumented immigrants residing in the US to gain legal status.

Healthcare

Keep core programs, lower costs, and make hospital pricing less like confusing, unpredictable, and unaffordable for ordinary families.

Bullet points
  • Keep programs such as Obamacare and Medicaid.
  • Lower healthcare costs, increase transparency, and protect people from extreme emergency hospital bills.
  • Require hospitals to publish clear prices for common procedures.
  • Protect rural hospitals for countrywide access.
  • Make necessary emergency surgery and emergency care cheaper for low-income individuals.
  • Increase competition between hospitals to lower prices.
