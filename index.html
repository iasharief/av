Hurricane Odette Notice

In the wake of this hurricane, the American Vanguard is committed to one principle above all else: show up as a country, no matter what. We will immediately mobilize resources, filtered water, fuel, and emergency energy supplies, directly to Florida's hardest-hit communities, cutting red tape so that aid moves fast and lands on the ground where it is needed most. Insurance companies that canceled policies before Hurricane Odette will be held accountable and required to make their policyholders whole. For uninsured residents, FEMA will prioritize them first, helping families reunite before anything else. We propose increasing federal funding not just to the military, but to all national security institutions, FEMA, the National Guard, and the emergency responders who keep Americans safe, while deploying AI-assisted military drones over affected areas to locate survivors. To recruit and retain those responders, we will offer housing down payment assistance to anyone who joins FEMA or the National Guard, the same commitment we make to our military. Any business that steps up to provide volunteers, clean water, fuel, or energy to affected residents will receive meaningful tax relief in the years ahead, because businesses that strengthen America should grow alongside it. We are calling on every state in this union to contribute hospital capacity and relief resources. This must be a national effort. For elections disrupted by the storm, we will deploy mail-in ballots and mobile polling units so that no citizen loses their voice. On climate: we recognize this threat is real, and we are actively investing in nuclear and clean energy, but the most urgent task right now is protecting lives. The hardest tension we faced was choosing between long-term solutions and immediate survival. We chose people first. Florida will not recover alone, because under the American Vanguard, no American ever does.

The point

America needs the long game again.

Modern politicians try to claim quick wins that kick the can down the road in order to win the next election. American Vanguard believes success should be measured by whether the country is stronger, safer, freer, and more independent in the long run.

The United States should lead the world because the alternative is worse. But our leadership will not enter a frenzy of endless wars, or assuming that every crisis needs American troops on the front line. If force is used, it needs a transparent goal, a real reason, and a clear exit.
